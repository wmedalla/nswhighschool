February Lesson Plan

Phase 1: Baseline Diagnostic & Word Problem Mastery • Year 5 Cohort

📅 Week-by-Week Breakdown

Week 1: Baseline Diagnostic Assessment

  • Monday (Reading & Vocab): Sit an untimed or lightly timed baseline diagnostic reading comprehension paper. Introduce 5 new Tier-2 words (e.g., audacious, coherent, tangible).
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Analyze baseline math errors; focus on core arithmetic speed.
  • Wednesday (Reasoning): Review baseline logic and pattern mistakes.
  • Thursday (Writing): Write a short unassisted persuasive response to establish a baseline writing style.
  • Saturday (Review): Collaborative review of baseline errors to map out focus areas for the term.

Week 2: Non-Fiction Analysis & Multi-Step Math

  • Monday (Reading): Comparative non-fiction analysis (extracting facts and author viewpoints across two articles).
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Multi-step word problems involving decimals and money calculations (calculator-free).
  • Wednesday (Reasoning): Grid alignment and directional shift puzzles.
  • Thursday (Writing): Crafting strong topic sentences and supporting body paragraphs.
  • Saturday (Review): Vocabulary flashcard review session.

Week 3: Inference & Ratio Applications

  • Monday (Reading): Deep inference practice—interpreting character motives and subtext in narrative extracts. Add 5 new vocabulary words.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Scaling ratios up and down (e.g., recipe scaling and word problem proportions).
  • Wednesday (Reasoning): Advanced number sequence gaps with multi-operation rules.
  • Thursday (Writing): Narrative tension—building up to a turning point.
  • Saturday (Review): Interactive quiz on ratio word problems.

Week 4: Month-End Consolidation

  • Monday (Reading): Mixed comprehension test focusing on vocabulary in context.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Mental arithmetic speed check (accuracy focus).
  • Wednesday (Reasoning): Logical sequence matching exercises.
  • Thursday (Writing): Editing a sample paragraph for punctuation and spelling errors.
  • Saturday (Review): Friendly, low-stress review of the month's milestones.
