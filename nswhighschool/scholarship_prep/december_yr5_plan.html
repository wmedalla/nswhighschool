December (Year 5) Lesson Plan

Phase 3: Holiday Consolidation & Complex Logic Puzzles • Year 5 Cohort

📅 Week-by-Week Breakdown

Week 1: Advanced Vocabulary & Percentage Scaling

  • Monday (Reading & Vocab): Deep-dive into root words, prefixes, and suffixes. Introduce 5 new Tier-2 words (e.g., candid, ubiquitous, zealous).
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Calculator-free percentage increase/decrease and profit/loss word problems.
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Complex matrix and scheduling logic puzzles.
  • Thursday (Writing): Persuasive essay drafting focusing on strong thesis integration.
  • Saturday (Review): Vocabulary flashcards and math speed drills.

Week 2: Humanities Extraction & Spatial Matrices

  • Monday (Reading): Humanities poetry and prose extraction practice.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Geometry and multi-step angle problem challenge sets.
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Spatial rotation and 3D cube net puzzles.
  • Thursday (Writing): Timed 25-minute persuasive writing practice.
  • Saturday (Review): Collaborative puzzle troubleshooting session.

Week 3: Digital Screen-Based Reading Simulation

  • Monday (Reading): Screen-based reading comprehension practice. Add 5 new vocabulary words.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Screen-based calculator-free math reasoning timed drills.
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Argument strengthening and weakening drills.
  • Thursday (Writing): Narrative pacing and descriptive hook refinement.
  • Saturday (Review): Screen-reading reflection and error log check.

Week 4: Holiday Light Review & Maintenance

  • Monday (Reading): Short mixed reading comprehension passage.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Mental math speed check.
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Logic puzzle review.
  • Thursday (Writing): Write a short holiday diary entry using elevated vocabulary.
  • Saturday (Review): Relaxed review heading into the holiday break.
