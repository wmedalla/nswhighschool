December Lesson Plan

Phase 1: Foundation & Ratio/Hooks • Year 4 Cohort

📅 Week-by-Week Breakdown

Week 1: Fiction Inference & Ratio Intro

  • Monday (Reading & Vocab): Focus on inferential questions (reading between the lines of a dialogue). Add 5 new Tier-2 words (e.g., ambiguous, cynical, desolate).
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Introduce simple ratio and proportion (sharing quantities in a given ratio, calculator-free).
  • Wednesday (Reasoning): Numerical sequence gaps (finding the missing number in alternating addition/multiplication series).
  • Thursday (Writing): Crafting engaging narrative hooks (starting with dialogue or an intriguing action rather than "It was a sunny day").
  • Saturday (Review): 30-minute review of vocabulary flashcards and ratio word problems.

Week 2: Informational Texts & Percentages

  • Monday (Reading): Reading historical or biographical articles; practice extracting main ideas from paragraphs.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Basic percentage calculations of whole numbers (e.g., 10%, 25%, 50%, 75%) without calculators.
  • Wednesday (Reasoning): Shape pattern shifts involving rotational symmetry and mirroring.
  • Thursday (Writing): Persuasive essay outlines—introducing strong opening statements and structural mapping.
  • Saturday (Review): Quick interactive quiz matching vocabulary terms to definitions.

Week 3: Figurative Language & Word Problems

  • Monday (Reading): Analyzing similes, metaphors, and personification in short poetry extracts. Add 5 new vocabulary words.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Multi-step addition and subtraction word problems involving money and time tables.
  • Wednesday (Reasoning): Grid-based spatial alignment puzzles and directional reasoning (north, south, east, west shifts).
  • Thursday (Writing): Narrative tension—building suspense during the middle obstacle of a story.
  • Saturday (Review): Collaborative reading aloud session focusing on expressive tone and pacing.

Week 4: Holiday Light Consolidation

  • Monday (Reading): Short mixed reading comprehension passage.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Mental arithmetic speed drills (focusing on accuracy over speed).
  • Wednesday (Reasoning): Light logic puzzle practice (identifying simple true/false statements).
  • Thursday (Writing): Write a short descriptive holiday diary entry using sensory adjectives.
  • Saturday (Review): Relaxed review of the month's learning milestones ahead of the holiday break.
