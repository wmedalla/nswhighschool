February (Year 6) Lesson Plan

Peak Prep: Application Window & Rigorous Mock Simulations • Year 6 Cohort

📅 Week-by-Week Breakdown

Week 1: Fortnightly Mock Simulation

  • Saturday (Simulation): Sit a full-length, back-to-back timed mock exam under strict test conditions. Introduce 5 new Tier-2 words (e.g., audacious, meticulous, resilient).
  • Monday–Wednesday (Review): Comprehensive error log analysis and categorization of persistent mistakes.
  • Thursday (Writing): Rewrite weak essay sections identified in the mock.

Week 2: Targeted Remediation & Speed Arithmetic

  • Monday (Reading): Comprehension drills focusing on weakest question types.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Calculator-free speed arithmetic drills.
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Logic puzzle and spatial rotation review.
  • Thursday (Writing): Timed 25-minute persuasive writing practice.
  • Saturday (Review): Vocabulary flashcard review.

Week 3: Fortnightly Mock Simulation & Application Check

  • Saturday (Simulation): Sit the second full-length mock exam of the month. Add 5 new vocabulary words.
  • Administrative Note: Ensure all NSW Selective and scholarship applications are finalized and submitted.
  • Monday–Wednesday (Review): Deep-dive error analysis focusing on time management and pacing.
  • Thursday (Writing): Narrative pacing and dialogue formatting practice.

Week 4: Month-End Error Consolidation

  • Monday (Reading): Mixed reading comprehension practice.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Multi-step word problem review.
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Reasoning review session.
  • Thursday (Writing): Essay outline speed practice.
  • Saturday (Review): Celebratory review of consistency and stamina gains.
