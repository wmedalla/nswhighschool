August Lesson Plan

Phase 3: First Full-Length Timed Mock Exams & Error Analysis • Year 5 Cohort

📅 Week-by-Week Breakdown

Week 1: First Full-Length Timed Mock Exam

  • Saturday/Weekend (Simulation): Sit the first full-length, timed mock exam across all disciplines under strict test conditions. Introduce 5 new Tier-2 words (e.g., audacious, benevolent, concise).
  • Monday–Wednesday (Review): Deep-dive error analysis session. Review every missed question to categorize whether it was a conceptual gap, careless error, or time-pressure mistake.
  • Thursday (Writing): Review essay feedback and rewrite weak paragraphs.

Week 2: Targeted Remediation & Math Speed

  • Monday (Reading): Targeted comprehension drills focusing on the weakest sections identified in the mock exam.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Calculator-free math drills targeting specific arithmetic or word problem weaknesses.
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Reviewing logic puzzles and argument analysis errors from the mock.
  • Thursday (Writing): Timed 25-minute persuasive writing practice.
  • Saturday (Review): Vocabulary flashcards and error log review.

Week 3: Humanities Extraction & Spatial Logic

  • Monday (Reading): Advanced poetry and humanities prose extraction drills. Add 5 new vocabulary words.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Multi-step fraction and percentage word problem combinations.
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Advanced spatial rotation and deduction puzzles.
  • Thursday (Writing): Narrative pacing and descriptive hook refinement.
  • Saturday (Review): Collaborative puzzle-solving session.

Week 4: Month-End Consolidation

  • Monday (Reading): Timed digital screen-based reading practice.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Mental math speed check.
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Logical deduction mini-quiz.
  • Thursday (Writing): Write a short response to an abstract narrative prompt.
  • Saturday (Review): Friendly, low-stress review of the month's progress.
