March Lesson Plan

Phase 2: Transition & Formal Thinking Skills (Logic & Truth/Liar Puzzles) • Year 5 Cohort

📅 Week-by-Week Breakdown

Week 1: Intro to Formal Thinking Skills & Percentages

  • Monday (Reading & Vocab): Poetry analysis with focus on mood and figurative imagery. Introduce 5 new Tier-2 words (e.g., equivocal, pragmatic, salient).
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Percentage increase and decrease calculations without calculators.
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Introduction to logic puzzles—solving simple conditional statements ("If A happens, then B follows"). You can reference sample tasks like those on [Thinking Skills Sample Paper](https://selectiveschooltutoring.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Thinking-Skills-Sample-Paper.pdf).
  • Thursday (Writing): Structuring a persuasive essay with a clear thesis statement.
  • Saturday (Review): Vocabulary flashcards and logic puzzle walkthrough.

Week 2: Argument Analysis & Geometry

  • Monday (Reading): Identifying assumptions and main arguments in opinion editorials.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Angles in triangles, parallel lines, and polygon properties (calculator-free).
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Truth-teller and liar puzzles (figuring out who is telling the truth based on conflicting statements).
  • Thursday (Writing): Developing counter-arguments and rebuttals in persuasive writing.
  • Saturday (Review): Interactive logic puzzle practice.

Week 3: Subtext & Word Problem Efficiency

  • Monday (Reading): Analyzing author tone and subtext in literary extracts. Add 5 new vocabulary words.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Speed arithmetic drills focusing on fractions, decimals, and percentages combined.
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Logical deduction based on seating arrangements and age/order ranking (similar to puzzle formats found in the [Thinking Skills Sample Paper](https://selectiveschooltutoring.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Thinking-Skills-Sample-Paper.pdf)).
  • Thursday (Writing): Timed 15-minute quick-write brainstorming based on abstract prompts.
  • Saturday (Review): Collaborative whiteboard problem-solving session.

Week 4: Month-End Consolidation

  • Monday (Reading): Mixed reading comprehension practice across fiction and non-fiction.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Multi-step word problem review test.
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Mini-quiz covering conditional logic and truth/liar statements.
  • Thursday (Writing): Write a short, structured persuasive paragraph.
  • Saturday (Review): Celebratory review of logical reasoning progress.
