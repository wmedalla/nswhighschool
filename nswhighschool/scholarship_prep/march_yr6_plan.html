March (Year 6) Lesson Plan

Peak Prep: Final Polishing of Math Speed & Humanities Interpretation • Year 6 Cohort

📅 Week-by-Week Breakdown

Week 1: Full-Length Timed Mock Exam

  • Saturday (Simulation): Sit a full-length, back-to-back timed mock exam under strict test conditions. Introduce 5 new Tier-2 words (e.g., cogent, judicious, meticulous).
  • Monday–Wednesday (Review): Deep-dive error log analysis focusing on eliminating recurring careless mistakes.
  • Thursday (Writing): Polish introduction hooks and concluding paragraphs in timed essays.

Week 2: Advanced Humanities & Speed Arithmetic

  • Monday (Reading): High-difficulty poetry and prose extraction drills.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Calculator-free speed arithmetic across challenging multi-step word problems.
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Truth-teller, liar, and formal logic puzzle masterclass.
  • Thursday (Writing): ACER Dual-Writing Simulation (2 back-to-back 25-minute writing tasks).
  • Saturday (Review): Vocabulary flashcards and writing structure review.

Week 3: Digital Screen Simulation & Spatial Logic

  • Monday (Reading): Digital screen-based reading comprehension practice. Add 5 new vocabulary words.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Screen-based calculator-free math reasoning timed drills.
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Advanced spatial rotation and scheduling puzzle practice.
  • Thursday (Writing): Timed persuasive essay drafting session.
  • Saturday (Review): Screen-reading reflection and error log check.

Week 4: Month-End Sectional Mock

  • Monday (Reading): Timed sectional reading mock.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Timed sectional math mock.
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Timed sectional reasoning mock.
  • Thursday (Writing): Timed essay practice.
  • Saturday (Review): Collaborative error analysis across the sectional mock.
