April (Year 6) Lesson Plan

Phase 4: Tapering Test Volume, Confidence Building & Logistics • Year 6 Cohort

📅 Week-by-Week Breakdown

Week 1: Tapering & Reducing Test Volume (50% Cut)

  • Monday (Reading & Vocab): Light vocabulary review and relaxed reading comprehension. Introduce 5 final Tier-2 words (e.g., pragmatic, resilient, tenacious).
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Mental math warm-ups (no intense timed drills).
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Reviewing favourite logic puzzle types for enjoyment rather than pressure.
  • Thursday (Writing): Quick essay structure and outline check.
  • Saturday (Review): Light review session focusing purely on past wins and confidence building.

Week 2: Targeted Error Log Refinement

  • Monday (Reading): Quick check on previously missed comprehension concepts.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Brief review of core formula sheets and fraction/percentage tricks.
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Relaxed spatial reasoning walkthrough.
  • Thursday (Writing): Writing a strong opening hook practice.
  • Saturday (Review): Error log review without time limits.

Week 3: Single Mock Simulation & Pacing Check

  • Saturday (Simulation): Run a final single, relaxed mock assessment to check timing and mental readiness.
  • Monday–Wednesday (Review): Gentle debrief focusing on maintaining calm and avoiding burnout.
  • Thursday (Writing): Final timed essay polish.

Week 4: Logistics & Exam Preparation Check

  • Administrative: Confirm exam venue locations, printing out required ticket/identification documents, and checking travel routes.
  • Mindset: Shift focus completely toward healthy sleep routines, nutritious meals, and protecting downtime.
  • Saturday (Review): Light flashcard scan and total relaxation.
