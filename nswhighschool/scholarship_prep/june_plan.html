June Lesson Plan

Phase 2: Digital Selective Adaptation & Advanced Logic • Year 5 Cohort

📅 Week-by-Week Breakdown

Week 1: Digital Screen-Based Reading Practice

  • Monday (Reading & Vocab): Practice reading scrollable text blocks and answering multiple-choice questions directly on a computer screen (simulating the Cambridge Selective platform). Introduce 5 new Tier-2 words (e.g., candor, ephemeral, pragmatic).
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Calculator-free mental math speed tests focusing on speed and accuracy under light time limits.
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Advanced logical deduction and classification puzzles.
  • Thursday (Writing): Persuasive essay structuring—crafting compelling counter-arguments.
  • Saturday (Review): Vocabulary flashcards and screen-reading reflection.

Week 2: Algebraic Reasoning & Geometry Word Problems

  • Monday (Reading): Identifying author intent and subtle bias in digital editorial extracts.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Geometry challenge problems—composite shapes and angle theorems without calculators.
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Complex truth-teller and liar logic puzzles.
  • Thursday (Writing): Timed 20-minute persuasive essay drafting session.
  • Saturday (Review): Interactive word problem troubleshooting.

Week 3: Humanities Inference & Spatial Logic

  • Monday (Reading): Advanced poetry and prose inference drills. Add 5 new vocabulary words.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Multi-step fraction, ratio, and percentage word problem combinations.
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Spatial rotation and 3D shape cube net puzzles.
  • Thursday (Writing): Narrative tension and descriptive pacing.
  • Saturday (Review): Collaborative whiteboard puzzle session.

Week 4: Month-End Consolidation

  • Monday (Reading): Digital screen-based mixed reading comprehension test.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Arithmetic speed check.
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Review of missed logical reasoning questions.
  • Thursday (Writing): Editing a sample essay for vocabulary elevation.
  • Saturday (Review): Friendly, low-stress review of the month's progress.
