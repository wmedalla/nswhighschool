Master Dual-Preparation Roadmap

Granular Day-by-Day & Week-by-Week Lesson Plans (Nov Year 4 – May Year 6)

November Foundation

Launch vocabulary, baseline reading structure, mental math fluency, and basic sentence skeletons.

December Foundation

Ratio calculations, descriptive writing hooks, and initial numerical series patterns.

January Foundation

Poetry analysis, geometry (area/perimeter), and matrix grid logic puzzles.

February Diagnostic

Baseline diagnostic assessment, identifying initial gaps, and targeted word-problem remediation.

March Transition

Introduction to formal Thinking Skills, truth/liar logic puzzles, and argument assumptions.

April Transition

Spatial reasoning, shape rotations, persuasive essay structuring, and mid-point review.

May Transition

ACER dual-writing bursts (2x25 min tasks), speed arithmetic drills, and humanities extraction.

June Transition

Digital Selective platform adaptation, advanced classification logic, and error log reviews.

July Transition

Mid-year consolidation, multi-step word problem mastery, and advanced vocabulary building.

August Simulation

First full-length timed mock exams, deep-dive error analysis, and pacing strategy adjustment.

September Simulation

Targeted remediation on weak sub-topics and alternating paper ACER vs. digital Selective drills.

October Simulation

Building test stamina, managing difficult multiple-choice questions, and advanced essay editing.

November (Year 5) Simulation

Fortnightly simulated exam blocks and continuous performance tracking.

December (Year 5) Simulation

Holiday consolidation, vocabulary deep dives, and complex logic puzzle mastery.

January (Year 6) Peak Prep

Intensive exam-condition practice, time pressure calibration, and advanced writing refinement.

February (Year 6) Peak Prep

Selective applications closing window, rigorous mock simulations, and targeted error fixes.

March (Year 6) Peak Prep

Final polishing of math speed, humanities interpretation, and reasoning accuracy.

April (Year 6) Tapering

Reducing test volume, light warm-ups, confidence boosting, and logistics check.

May (Year 6) Exam Window

Final taper, rest schedules, and sitting the ACER and NSW Selective exams with total confidence!
