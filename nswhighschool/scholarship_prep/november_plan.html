November Lesson Plan

Phase 1: Foundation & Launch • Year 4 Cohort

📅 Week-by-Week Breakdown

Week 1: Structural Reading & Basic Vocab

  • Monday (Reading & Vocab): Read a short narrative excerpt; identify the setting, character motivations, and introduce 5 new Tier-2 words (e.g., benevolent, meticulous, resilient).
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Calculator-free mental math drills focusing on fast addition/subtraction bridges and simple fraction equivalents.
  • Wednesday (Reasoning): Basic number series and counting patterns (+3, -2, alternate sequences).
  • Thursday (Writing): Introduce the 3-part narrative skeleton (Beginning hook, Middle obstacle, End resolution).
  • Saturday (Review): 30-minute relaxed review of the week's vocabulary flashcards and math drills.

Week 2: Non-Fiction & Fractions

  • Monday (Reading): Read a simple non-fiction article on science or nature; highlight key facts vs. opinions.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Converting improper fractions to mixed numbers and simple fraction-to-decimal mental conversions.
  • Wednesday (Reasoning): Shape pattern sequences (identifying rotation and shading shifts).
  • Thursday (Writing): Practicing descriptive openings using sensory details (sight, sound, touch).
  • Saturday (Review): Collaborative marker-pen exercise mapping a short story structure.

Week 3: Author Tone & Division Fluency

  • Monday (Reading): Identify author tone (humorous, serious, persuasive) in short editorial snippets. Add 5 new vocabulary words.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Calculator-free long division practice and remainder interpretation word problems.
  • Wednesday (Reasoning): Alphabetical coding and simple letter-shift substitution puzzles.
  • Thursday (Writing): Persuasive writing structure—stating a clear opinion with two supporting reasons.
  • Saturday (Review): Short 15-minute quiz on vocabulary words learned so far.

Week 4: Consolidation & Mini-Checkpoint

  • Monday (Reading): Mixed comprehension practice (fiction + non-fiction extracts).
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Mental arithmetic speed test (untimed, focusing on accuracy).
  • Wednesday (Reasoning): Reviewing missed reasoning patterns from Weeks 1–3.
  • Thursday (Writing): Write a short 150-word story based on a picture prompt.
  • Saturday (Review): Friendly, low-stress review of the month's progress to celebrate consistency.
