July Lesson Plan

Phase 2: Mid-Year Consolidation & Multi-Step Word Problems • Year 5 Cohort

📅 Week-by-Week Breakdown

Week 1: Vocabulary Deep-Dive & Speed Math

  • Monday (Reading & Vocab): Deep-dive into root words, prefixes, and suffixes. Introduce 5 new Tier-2 words (e.g., fastidious, gregarious, innocuous).
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Speed arithmetic drills focusing on reducing calculation errors under time pressure.
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Argument strengthening and weakening drills (analyzing evidence strength).
  • Thursday (Writing): Narrative writing—crafting immersive settings and atmospheric tone.
  • Saturday (Review): Vocabulary flashcard review and speed math check.

Week 2: Comparative Literature & Multi-Step Word Problems

  • Monday (Reading): Comparing two literary extracts for stylistic differences and word choice impact.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Multi-step word problem decomposition (breaking complex scenarios into simple arithmetic steps).
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Logical scheduling and timetable puzzles.
  • Thursday (Writing): Persuasive essay outlining and thesis statement refinement.
  • Saturday (Review): Interactive word problem walkthrough.

Week 3: Humanities Inference & Spatial Rotation

  • Monday (Reading): Humanities text extraction and distractor elimination practice. Add 5 new vocabulary words.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Advanced percentage, ratio, and proportion challenge sets.
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Spatial rotation and 2D/3D shape pattern matching.
  • Thursday (Writing): Timed 25-minute persuasive writing practice.
  • Saturday (Review): Collaborative puzzle session.

Week 4: Mid-Year Mini-Mock Review

  • Monday (Reading): Mini-mock reading comprehension passage (timed).
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Mini-mock calculator-free math section (timed).
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Mini-mock logic and reasoning section (timed).
  • Thursday (Writing): Reviewing essay structures and feedback.
  • Saturday (Review): Collaborative error analysis session across all mini-mock sections.
