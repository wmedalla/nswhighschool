May Lesson Plan

Phase 2: ACER Dual-Writing Bursts & Speed Arithmetic • Year 5 Cohort

📅 Week-by-Week Breakdown

Week 1: ACER Writing Stamina (Dual Tasks)

  • Monday (Reading & Vocab): Humanities poetry and prose extraction drills. Introduce 5 new Tier-2 words (e.g., altruistic, meticulous, stringent).
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Speed arithmetic drills targeting rapid calculation across fractions and decimals.
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Advanced deductive logic matrices and scheduling puzzles.
  • Thursday (Writing): **ACER Dual-Writing Simulation:** Practice writing two distinct pieces (one narrative, one persuasive) back-to-back within strict 25-minute limits.
  • Saturday (Review): Review writing structure and vocabulary flashcards.

Week 2: Humanities Comprehension & Data Interpretation

  • Monday (Reading): Historical and literary text extraction focusing on distractor elimination.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Interpreting tables, charts, and basic statistical word problems without calculators.
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Identifying underlying assumptions and strengthening/weakening arguments.
  • Thursday (Writing): Timed persuasive essay outline and vocabulary elevation.
  • Saturday (Review): Interactive math word problem speed run.

Week 3: Advanced Logic & Ratio Scaling

  • Monday (Reading): Analyzing figurative language and author voice in classic literature extracts. Add 5 new vocabulary words.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Complex proportion, speed-distance-time, and ratio multi-step problems.
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Complex truth-teller and liar logic puzzles.
  • Thursday (Writing): Narrative pacing and dialogue integration.
  • Saturday (Review): Collaborative logic puzzle walkthrough.

Week 4: Month-End Consolidation

  • Monday (Reading): Timed mixed reading comprehension passage.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Calculator-free mental math check.
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Review of missed logical reasoning questions.
  • Thursday (Writing): Write a short response to an abstract narrative image prompt.
  • Saturday (Review): Relaxed review of the month's writing stamina progress.
