April Lesson Plan

Phase 2: Spatial Reasoning, Shape Combinations & Persuasive Essays • Year 5 Cohort

📅 Week-by-Week Breakdown

Week 1: Spatial Reasoning & Decimals

  • Monday (Reading & Vocab): Analyzing persuasive texts and identifying emotional appeals. Introduce 5 new Tier-2 words (e.g., bolster, delineate, lucrative).
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Decimal operations (multiplying and dividing decimals by powers of 10, calculator-free).
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Spatial reasoning and shape combination puzzles (similar to combining puzzle pieces or tile transformations found in a [Thinking Skills Sample Paper](https://selectiveschooltutoring.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Thinking-Skills-Sample-Paper.pdf)).
  • Thursday (Writing): Structuring an argument body paragraph with a clear claim, evidence, and explanation.
  • Saturday (Review): Vocabulary review and spatial rotation drill practice.

Week 2: Perspective Analysis & Volume/Capacity

  • Monday (Reading): Comparing how two authors treat the same subject from different viewpoints.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Volume and capacity calculations for rectangular prisms (calculator-free).
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Complex ordering and ranking puzzles (e.g., age or race position logic).
  • Thursday (Writing): Drafting a complete persuasive essay within a loose 25-minute timer.
  • Saturday (Review): Interactive word problem troubleshooting.

Week 3: Subtext Inference & Logical Flaws

  • Monday (Reading): Deep inference practice extracting unstated assumptions in literature. Add 5 new vocabulary words.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Advanced multi-step fraction and percentage word problems.
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Identifying flaws in arguments (recognizing weak evidence or logical leaps).
  • Thursday (Writing): Narrative tension—writing a cliffhanger ending.
  • Saturday (Review): Collaborative whiteboard puzzle session.

Week 4: Mid-Point Mini-Review

  • Monday (Reading): Short mixed reading comprehension practice.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Mental math speed drill (accuracy focus).
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Mini-mock test reviewing spatial and logic questions.
  • Thursday (Writing): Peer-editing exercise (checking punctuation and paragraph flow).
  • Saturday (Review): Relaxed review celebrating the halfway mark of Year 5 preparation.
