May (Year 6) Lesson Plan

Peak & Exam Window: Final Taper, Rest Schedules & Exam Execution • Year 6 Cohort

📅 Week-by-Week Breakdown

Week 1: Final Taper & Light Warm-Ups

  • Monday–Thursday: Extremely light mental math and vocabulary card flips (10–15 minutes max per day). No heavy testing or new concept introduction.
  • Focus: Prioritizing early bedtimes, balanced nutrition, and keeping normal relaxing hobbies active.
  • Saturday: Relaxed family activity or day out to completely detach from study stress.

Week 2: Exam Window Execution (ACER & NSW Selective Tests)

  • Test Days: Sit the ACER scholarship tests and the NSW Selective High School Placement Test with total confidence!
  • Mindset Reminder: Trust the 18 months of consistent, structured preparation. Remind your daughter that her goal is simply to do her personal best.

Weeks 3 & 4: Post-Exam Celebration & Recovery

  • Decompression: Let go of all academic targets and focus entirely on celebration, rest, and rewarding all the hard work put in over the past year and a half.
