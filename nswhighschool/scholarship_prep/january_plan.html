January Lesson Plan

Phase 1: Poetry, Geometry & Logic Grids • Year 5 Cohort

📅 Week-by-Week Breakdown

Week 1: Poetry Structure & Perimeter/Area

  • Monday (Reading & Vocab): Analyze poetic form, rhyme scheme, and stanza structure. Introduce 5 new Tier-2 words (e.g., formidable, pragmatic, tentative).
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Calculating perimeter and area of composite rectangular shapes (calculator-free).
  • Wednesday (Reasoning): Matrix grid logic puzzles (identifying missing symbols in a 3x3 grid).
  • Thursday (Writing): Narrative conclusions—writing satisfying resolutions that tie up plot threads.
  • Saturday (Review): Review geometry formulas and vocabulary flashcards.

Week 2: Author Intent & Fractions/Decimals

  • Monday (Reading): Identifying author intent and subtle bias in persuasive op-ed columns.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Advanced fraction operations (adding and subtracting fractions with related denominators).
  • Wednesday (Reasoning): Logical deduction puzzles based on age or seating arrangements.
  • Thursday (Writing): Persuasive counter-arguments (addressing the opposing side respectfully).
  • Saturday (Review): Short vocabulary matching quiz.

Week 3: Tone Analysis & Word Problem Scaling

  • Monday (Reading): Comparing two texts with opposing tones on the same topic. Add 5 new vocabulary words.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Multi-step word problems involving speed, distance, and time ratios.
  • Wednesday (Reasoning): Alphabetical coding and letter-shifting puzzles.
  • Thursday (Writing): Quick 15-minute essay outline practice based on a prompt.
  • Saturday (Review): Collaborative whiteboard math puzzle session.

Week 4: Month-End Consolidation

  • Monday (Reading): Mixed comprehension test extracting literal and inferential details.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Mental math speed check (accuracy focus).
  • Wednesday (Reasoning): Reviewing missed logic patterns from Weeks 1–3.
  • Thursday (Writing): Draft a short narrative paragraph focusing on dialogue punctuation.
  • Saturday (Review): Celebratory review of progress entering Year 5.
