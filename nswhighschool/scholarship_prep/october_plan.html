October Lesson Plan

Phase 3: Test Stamina & Difficult Multiple-Choice Strategies • Year 5 Cohort

📅 Week-by-Week Breakdown

Week 1: Multiple-Choice Elimination Techniques

  • Monday (Reading & Vocab): Practicing distractor elimination techniques (identifying why incorrect options are traps). Introduce 5 new Tier-2 words (e.g., equivocal, meticulous, plausible).
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Managing difficult math questions ("knowing when to skip and return").
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Advanced argument evaluation and assumption spotting.
  • Thursday (Writing): Advanced essay editing—elevating vocabulary and varying sentence length.
  • Saturday (Review): Vocabulary flashcards and elimination strategy review.

Week 2: Full Sectional Timed Practice

  • Monday (Reading): Timed 30-minute reading comprehension paper.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Timed 30-minute calculator-free math paper.
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Timed 30-minute logic reasoning paper.
  • Thursday (Writing): Timed persuasive essay drafting.
  • Saturday (Review): Detailed error log analysis across all four sectional tests.

Week 3: Humanities Extraction & Spatial Puzzles

  • Monday (Reading): Advanced poetry and prose extraction drills. Add 5 new vocabulary words.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Complex multi-step word problem challenge sets.
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Spatial rotation and complex grid puzzle masterclass.
  • Thursday (Writing): Narrative tension and atmospheric hooks.
  • Saturday (Review): Collaborative puzzle troubleshooting.

Week 4: Month-End Mini-Mock

  • Monday (Reading): Digital screen-based reading comprehension review.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Mental math speed check.
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Truth-teller and liar puzzle review.
  • Thursday (Writing): Write a short response to an abstract narrative prompt.
  • Saturday (Review): Friendly, low-stress review of stamina progress.
