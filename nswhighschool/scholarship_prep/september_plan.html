September Lesson Plan

Phase 3: Targeted Remediation & Alternating ACER/Selective Drills • Year 5 Cohort

📅 Week-by-Week Breakdown

Week 1: ACER Paper Booklets & Dual Writing

  • Monday (Reading & Vocab): High-difficulty humanities extraction using paper booklets. Introduce 5 new Tier-2 words (e.g., deleterious, resilient, ubiquitous).
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Speed arithmetic drills across complex word problems.
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Truth-teller and liar logic puzzle masterclasses.
  • Thursday (Writing): ACER Dual-Writing Simulation (2 back-to-back 25-minute writing tasks).
  • Saturday (Review): Vocabulary flashcards and writing structure review.

Week 2: Digital Selective Platform Simulation

  • Monday (Reading): Digital screen-based reading comprehension practice.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Calculator-free math reasoning timed drills on screen.
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Advanced formal logic and scheduling puzzle practice.
  • Thursday (Writing): Persuasive essay outlining and vocabulary elevation.
  • Saturday (Review): Screen-reading reflection and error log check.

Week 3: Targeted Weak-Spot Remediation

  • Monday (Reading): Focusing exclusively on recurring inference or vocabulary errors. Add 5 new vocabulary words.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Isolating challenging math sub-topics (e.g., multi-step ratio or geometry problems).
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Reviewing spatial reasoning and argument flaw questions.
  • Thursday (Writing): Narrative pacing and dialogue formatting.
  • Saturday (Review): Collaborative whiteboard troubleshooting session.

Week 4: Month-End Sectional Mock

  • Monday (Reading): Timed sectional reading mock.
  • Tuesday (Numeracy): Timed sectional math mock.
  • Wednesday (Thinking Skills): Timed sectional reasoning mock.
  • Thursday (Writing): Timed essay practice.
  • Saturday (Review): Collaborative error analysis across the sectional mock.
