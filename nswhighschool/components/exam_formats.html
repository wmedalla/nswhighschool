NSW Exam Formats Deep Dive

Complete breakdown of the Selective High School Test and ACER Scholarship Exams

💻 NSW Selective High School Placement Test Format

Administered digitally on computers by Cambridge Assessment (Equal 25% weightings).

Reading

⏳ 45 Mins📊 25%

17 questions (~38 answers). Multiple choice testing literal comprehension, inference, and vocabulary.

Mathematical Reasoning

⏳ 40 Mins📊 25%

35 multiple-choice questions. Calculator-free problem solving and arithmetic.

Thinking Skills

⏳ 40 Mins📊 25%

40 multiple-choice logic, argument analysis, and abstract reasoning puzzles.

Writing

⏳ 30 Mins📊 25%

1 extended response typed directly on computer based on a stimulus prompt.

🎓 Private School Scholarship Exam Formats

Managed independently via external test providers like ACER, Edutest, or AAS.

1. ACER Format

  • Written Expression: 2 tasks (25 mins each).
  • Humanities: ~40–45 multiple-choice questions (40 mins).
  • Mathematics: ~32–36 questions, no calculators (40 mins).

2. Edutest Format

  • Verbal Reasoning, Numerical Reasoning, Reading, Mathematics (30 mins each).
  • Short-burst Writing (15 mins).

3. AAS Format

  • Reasoning & Problem Solving, Reading Comprehension, Mathematics (~40-45 mins each).
  • Written Expression (25-30 mins).

✏️ How to Prepare for the ACER Scholarship Exam

1. Written Expression

Practice both creative storytelling and persuasive essays. Focus on pacing (3m plan, 18m write, 4m edit) and rich vocabulary.

2. Humanities Comprehension

Build inferential reading skills across fiction, poetry, and editorials. Practice eliminating distractor answers.

3. Mathematics

Strengthen mental arithmetic (fractions, percentages, ratios) and multi-step word problem breakdowns without calculators.
