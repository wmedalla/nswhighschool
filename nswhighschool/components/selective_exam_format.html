💻 NSW Selective High School Placement Test Format

The exam is administered digitally on computers by Cambridge Assessment. Each of the four components contributes an equal 25% weighting to the final score.

Reading

⏳ 45 Minutes 📊 25% Weighting

Questions: 17 questions (~38 answers).

Format: Computer-based multiple choice with scrollable text extracts.

Mathematical Reasoning

⏳ 40 Minutes 📊 25% Weighting

Questions: 35 multiple-choice questions (Calculator-free).

Thinking Skills

⏳ 40 Minutes 📊 25% Weighting

Questions: 40 multiple-choice logic and abstract reasoning puzzles.

Writing

⏳ 30 Minutes 📊 25% Weighting

Task: 1 extended response typed directly on computer.
